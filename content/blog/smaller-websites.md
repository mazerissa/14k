# Why Websites Should Be Smaller

Most websites are much larger than they need to be.

A personal website doesn't need megabytes of JavaScript just to display some text.

## The 14 KB experiment

This website is an experiment in building a personal website with an extremely small runtime.

The idea is simple:

- Store content as data.
- Compress repeated information.
- Generate only the page the visitor requests.
- Generate graphics mathematically when possible.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

## The goal

The goal isn't simply to make a website small.

The goal is to understand how much can be done with very little code.

**Small is good.**
